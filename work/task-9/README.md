# Task 9: Insight Generation and Reporting

## Objective

Turn the analysis, comparisons and forecasts into clear, actionable insights, on a shared reporting structure.

## What is submitted

**Each member (in their own folder)**

1. A concise insight report and visual summaries
2. A short explanation of the company's hiring strategy and market position

## Rules for this task

- Every insight cites the artefact it comes from, so the mentor can trace it.
- Written for a strategy or HR reader, not for a data scientist.
- A limitations section is mandatory.
