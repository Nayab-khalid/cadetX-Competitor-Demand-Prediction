# Legal and Ethical Rules

Agreed by the team on: YYYY-MM-DD

These rules apply to every member for the whole project.

## 1. Permitted sources

Data is collected only from sources listed in `approved-sources.md`. Any new source must be
reviewed and approved by the team before it is used.

## 2. Terms of Service

The Terms of Service of every source are read before collection. The clause that permits or
restricts automated access, research use, or redistribution is quoted in the member's source review,
with the date it was read.

## 3. robots.txt

The robots.txt file of every website is checked before collection, and the relevant User-agent and
Disallow lines are quoted with the date of the check. If the relevant path is disallowed, the source
is not used. Bot protection, rate limits and paywalls are never bypassed.

## 4. Personal and sensitive data

No personal or sensitive data is collected, stored or committed at any point. This includes names,
email addresses, telephone numbers, recruiter identities and any other information that identifies
an individual. Only company-level job-posting information is collected.

## 5. Copyright

Factual fields such as job title, posting date and location are recorded. Full job description text
is used for analysis, and licence and attribution requirements are documented for every source. The
repository is not used to republish copyrighted content as a dataset release.

## 6. Provenance

For every dataset, the source, the exact URL, the access date and the collection method are
recorded in the member's Data Collection Report in Task 2.

## 7. Changes

If the terms of a source change during the project, use of that source stops immediately and the
decision is recorded in the meeting minutes.
