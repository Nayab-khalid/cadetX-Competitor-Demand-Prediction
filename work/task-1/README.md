# Task 1: Understanding Data Sources and Legal

## Objective

Identify where job-posting data can legally be obtained, and establish the legal and ethical rules
the team will follow for the rest of the project. No data is collected in this task.

Sources to consider: public datasets, open-data portals, Kaggle, GitHub, and company career pages.
For each one the team must understand the Terms of Service, the robots.txt rules, and the difference
between publicly available and copyrighted data.

## What is submitted

**Team (in `team/`)**

1. An approved list of data sources: `team/approved-sources.md`
2. A written statement of the legal requirements the team will follow: `team/legal-rules.md`
3. A shared plan for the data collection in Task 2: `team/collection-plan.md`

**Each member (in their own folder)**

1. A source review for their company: `source-review.md`

## Rules agreed for this task

- A source is only approved once every item in [docs/legal-checklist.md](../../docs/legal-checklist.md)
  has been checked and dated.
- No personal or sensitive data is collected by anyone at any stage of this project.
- If the Terms of Service or robots.txt of a source are unclear, the source is rejected and an
  alternative is chosen.
- Every member records the exact source they will use in Task 2, so there is no duplicated or
  missing work.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Every candidate source has a completed and dated checklist
- [ ] `team/approved-sources.md` is agreed by all four members
- [ ] `team/legal-rules.md` and `team/collection-plan.md` are complete
- [ ] Each member has committed their `source-review.md`
- [ ] The decision is recorded in the sprint 1 meeting minutes
