# Minutes of Meeting

One folder per sprint. The Scrum Leader of the week uploads the minutes of the weekly Microsoft
Teams meeting into the folder for that sprint, on the day of the meeting.

Each folder contains:

- `minutes.md`, recording attendance, roles, progress, decisions, blockers and next steps
- each member's own weekly note, named `note-<member>.md`, recording what they did that week

The role rota is in [role-rota.md](role-rota.md).
